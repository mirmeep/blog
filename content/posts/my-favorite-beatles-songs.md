### I have too many favorite Beatles songs, so I thought I’d narrow it down by category:

My favorite Beatles song that John wrote- Across the Universe

My favorite Beatles song that Paul wrote- Let it Be

My favorite Beatles song that George wrote- While My Guitar Gently Weeps

My favorite Beatles song that Ringo wrote- Octopus's Garden

My favorite Beatles song with a string quartet- Eleanor Rigby

My favorite Beatles song about casual mass murder- Maxwell’s Silver Hammer

My favorite Beatles song that makes fun of The Beach Boys- Back to the USSR

My favorite Beatles song about Paul’s dog- Martha my Dear

My favorite Beatles song where Lennon throws a party for a classical orchestra and dresses them all in clown costumes so as to loosen them up- A Day in the Life

My favorite Beatles song that initially got banned from the radio- With a Little Help From my Friends

My favorite Beatles song that was inspired by two monkeys having fun in the middle of the street in India- Why Don't We Do it in the Road

My favorite Beatles song about an implied oedipus complex- Julia

My favorite Beatles song where a piano solo is sped up so as to simulate a harpsichord- In My Life

My favorite Beatles song that was pure experimentation- Revolution 9

My favorite Beatles song which is argued to be the first metal song- Helter Skelter

My favorite Beatles song which is argued to be the first *heavy* metal song- She's so Heavy

My favorite Beatles song that is accurate for new university graduates to this day- You Never Give me your Money

My favorite Beatles song where Paul is a walrus (but is he dead?)- I am the Walrus

My favorite Beatles song about an oddly specific story about cowboys- Rocky Raccoon

My favorite Beatles song about “women”splaining- She Said She Said

My favorite Beatles song named after a street in the Hollywood hills- Blue Jay Way

My favorite Beatles song which gives a solid summary on the discography of the Beatles- Glass Onion

My favorite Beatles song where Paul can’t count- Eight Days a Week

My favorite Beatles song that is just too real- I’m so Tired

And, last but not least, my favorite Beatles song where no one ever expects it- Her Majesty

### I hope that clears any confusion over my favorite Beatles songs.
