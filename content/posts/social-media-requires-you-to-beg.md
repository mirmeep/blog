With the recent wave of Instagram bans, Meta has just established a new rule that, in order to submit an appeal, you have to get on your knees and beg.

”The only way we can tell whether someone is a bot is if we see them lick our shoes,” says Instagram spokesperson Martin Smith. “There’s a ton of accounts out there pretending to be real people, and that’s what Meta is all about. *Real* people. It’s Facebook, not Botbook for crying out loud!” 

Since then, Meta has come out with a list of requirements that each banned user has to complete to prove they’re not a bot. This includes sending Meta representatives out into the world to interact with people.

“It’s actually been really good for our workforce, with all these layoffs due to AI, you know,” says Smith. “Our employees can go into the field and make contact with humans. Of course, we still have to go through with protocol in order to actually *determine* if they’re human or not.”

But many people are unhappy by this prospect. 

“It’s an invasion of privacy!” says Tom Peterson from Idaho whose business account on candle making got banned last month. “They come into your house, force you to lay down, and they pop a squat over your chest just to lay a hot steamy one!” 

One business owner, who wishes to remain anonymous, from Oakland, California claimed that their Meta representative broke through their back gate, whipped out his penis, and threatened that if he doesn’t ejaculate in their face *right now*, their account will be permanently banned. “I mean, what was I supposed to do? Just let a decade of marketing go to waste?”

Fortunately, these two businesses won their appeals. 

However, several Instagram users were not as lucky. One business owner based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania had to go through weeks of torture by being tied to her own basement wall. “These guys are thorough, I’m telling you,” said a distressed Ava Pierce, whose business is in book binding. “They brought handcuffs, leather harnesses, a dog collar, and every whip you could possibly imagine! It doesn’t even guarantee your account will be permitted. Just a waste of time! Years of marketing content gone, just like that.”

In the wake of AI, Meta decided to create this thorough process to remind us that there is more to life than AI. 

“Oh yes, we pride ourselves in the creative initiative that each of our representatives take,” says Smith. “There’s just something satisfying that even AI can’t accomplish. It reminds us that we are human.”
